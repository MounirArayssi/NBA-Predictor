Pipeline · features · guardrails

How it works

One command runs the daily cycle. Everything is stored in PostgreSQL so every prediction can be traced back to the exact inputs it used.

  1. STEP 1Games & scoresSchedule, final scores and box scores from the NBA stats API.
  2. STEP 2Injury reportThe official NBA injury report PDF, parsed into who is Out or Doubtful and how many points per game that removes.
  3. STEP 3Team formRolling 5/7/10/20-game offensive and defensive ratings and pace for every team.
  4. STEP 4Team strengthA margin-of-victory Elo rating plus a season-to-date margin blended with last season early on.
  5. STEP 5PredictGradient-boosted models predict each team's score; the margin gives the pick and the win probability.
  6. STEP 6Lock & gradeThe pre-game prediction is locked as official, then scored against the final once the game ends.

What the model listens to

features. Share of the model's splits, averaged over the home and away score models.

Guardrails

  • No betting lines in the prediction. Odds are stored only to grade the model against the market later.
  • Same injury report it would have had. Training and backtests use the official report from 5:30 PM ET on game day, never who actually sat out.
  • Train and live match. Live features are built by the same code as training; Elo values are checked to match exactly.
  • Time only moves forward. Backtests never train on a game from the season being predicted.
  • No post-game picks. A prediction made after tip-off can never become official.

Research log

  • Nov 7, 2026
    NBA Cup games: are they higher scoring?No effect

    Point differential is a Cup tiebreaker, so teams might keep starters in. Across 180 group-stage games (2023-24 to 2025-26), Cup games averaged 229.0 points against 229.8 for regular games in the same weeks, and teams leading by 15+ after three quarters gave back about as much in the fourth. Not added as a feature.

  • Nov 7, 2026
    Retrained on five seasons of historyKept

    Elo and training now start in October 2021 instead of 2023. On held-out seasons it matched or beat the shorter history: 2025-26 winners 65.3% → 67.0%, with lower margin and total error in two of three test seasons.

  • Nov 6, 2026
    Walk-forward backtest of four seasonsKept

    Filled the data gaps first: 209 days of 2022-23 injury reports, 260 missing box scores and team form back to 2021. Then predicted 5,254 games, each season with a model that had never seen it.

  • Nov 6, 2026
    Does home court matter less when the visitor is clearly better?Open

    Mixed so far. In 2022-23 road favorites won by less than predicted; early 2023-24 showed the opposite. With two or more seasons of training, predicted margins line up with actual margins overall. To be tested as a feature.

  • Nov 5, 2026
    Removed betting lines from the predictionKept

    The model used to lean toward the Vegas spread and total. Now it stands on its own, so it can be compared with the market honestly.

  • Nov 5, 2026
    Elo + season blend, lean 21-feature modelKept

    Replaced a 42-feature set. Out of sample on 2026-27: margin miss 12.63 → 11.64, winners 59.5% → 61.2%, Brier 0.244 → 0.223. Shooting splits, matchup products and scoring variance added nothing once team strength was measured well.

  • Nov 5, 2026
    Schedule load, travel and per-team home courtNo gain

    Games in the last four or six nights, travel distance, time zones and team-specific home-court strength were each tested and did not improve accuracy.

  • Nov 5, 2026
    Team model only, player model kept for displayKept

    Blending in summed player predictions cost winners in every slice tested (this season, February to April, playoffs). Player-level information now enters through the injury feature instead.

Next experiments

Roadmap

Each idea gets the same test before it reaches the live model: the four-season walk-forward backtest.

Model structure

Predict margin and total directly

Two separate team-score models optimize the wrong targets. A margin model and a total model match what gets graded: the winner, the spread and the over/under.

Injuries

Lineup-level strength

Use on/off ratings of who is actually available instead of points per game missing. Losing a defensive center hurts more than his scoring says.

Market

Does the market overreact to schedule spots?

Back-to-backs and travel didn't improve accuracy, but the betting line may still misprice them. Needs historical odds.

Injuries

Questionable as a probability

Count a Questionable player as partly missing, weighted by how often players with that tag actually sit.

Win probability

Recalibrate on 5,000 games

Refit the win-probability curve on the walk-forward results instead of a few dozen live games.

Grading

Historical odds and closing-line value

Backfill opening and closing lines to measure where the model disagrees with Vegas and who was closer.
