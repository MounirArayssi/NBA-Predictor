Stars play through a Questionable tag more often than bench players: 65% for 20-point scorers against 39% for players under 8. That matters because the missing-points input is weighted by scoring, so the players who most affect a forecast are also the most likely to play.

The rate is steady across seasons, between about 45% and 55%, so a single historical estimate is reasonable.

## Next

Two options to test against each other in walk-forward cross-validation:

1. **Partial absence.** Count a Questionable player as partly missing, weighted by how often players in his scoring tier sit (about 35% for 20-point scorers, about 60% for players under 8).
2. **Separate input.** Give the model a `questionable_pts` input next to `missing_pts` and let it learn the weight.

A third idea depends on timing rather than modeling: rerun each forecast closer to tip-off, after most Questionable players have been confirmed in or out.
